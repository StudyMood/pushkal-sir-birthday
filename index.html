Career Roadmap

The Intern-To-Employee Journey

How Pushkal Sir carefully architects every phase of an intern's transformation into a skilled professional.

Phase 01 • Day One

New Intern Joins Digicoders

A fresh graduate enters with uncertainty and questions. Pushkal Sir greets them with warmth, sets clear goals, and creates a welcoming, fear-free learning atmosphere.

Phase 02 • Foundation

Immersive Learning & Basics

Structured daily tasks, foundational technical guidance, and collaborative exercises designed to turn raw theoretical knowledge into practical logic.

Phase 03 • Application

Real-World Project Contribution

Interns get their hands dirty on live modules. Under Pushkal Sir's close code reviews, they learn debugging, clean architecture, and git collaboration.

Phase 04 • Confidence

Problem Solving & Autonomy

Interns begin independently diagnosing bugs, proposing features, and communicating with clients, gaining immense professional maturity.

Phase 05 • Graduation

Interns successfully evolve into full-fledged software engineers and team leaders, ready to conquer any challenge in the global tech landscape.
